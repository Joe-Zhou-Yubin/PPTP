🎨 Clusters

Detected via spectral bisection on the modularity matrix. The algorithm finds the axis along which the network splits most naturally — people on opposite sides interact more within their group than across groups. It recursively splits the largest group until exactly 5 clusters emerge. This is the same maths behind Moretti's observation that removing Hamlet reveals the Court vs. anti-Court divide.

How it works
  1. Modularity matrix — For each pair of people, compute: actual connection − expected connection (based on how popular each person is). This highlights surprising connections.
  2. Power iteration — Find the direction along which the modularity signal is strongest. People with opposite signs on this axis belong to different communities.
  3. Recursive split — Split the largest group, repeat until we have 5 groups.
  4. Merge tiny groups — If isolated nodes form extra groups, fold them into whichever group they're closest to.
